Day 1 · Mon Sep 21 · to Bromont

Morning: REM from Édouard-Montpetit toward Brossard (~20–25 min). Non-electric bikes OK all hours through Sep 27.

SegmentKmNotes
REM Brossard → Chambly~15–25Montée / Riveraine. Normandie detour if La Marina passerelle is closed.
Chambly Canal → Saint-Jean~20Flat. Bridge 4 closed: use Bridge 7 / Chemin du Canal.
Saint-Jean → Farnham → Granby junction~48Montérégiade. Farnham Magenta detour; Antonio-Bernier if needed.
Estriade / Villageoise → Bromont~13–17Asphalt into town.

Stops: Chambly (coffee / Fort), Saint-Jean (snack), Farnham lunch (Yamaska Café or Fromagerie area), Bromont tourist office (15 boul. de Bromont) on arrival.

Day 2 · Tue Sep 22 · Bromont → Granby

Shorter scenic day (~48.1 km) on Estriade via Waterloo. Stops short of Magog / Orford.

Stops: Relais des Cheminots, Waterloo café if you go that far, Café de la Brûlerie in Granby.

Day 3 · Wed Sep 23 · Granby → Brossard REM

About 66.5 km west on Montérégiade + canal from Castel. Same Farnham Magenta detour. REM home anytime afternoon/evening.

SegmentKmNotes

Saint-Jean → Chambly~20Canal.
Chambly → Brossard REM~15–25Montée / Riveraine.

Stops: Farnham coffee if needed; Chambly late lunch before the final push.
